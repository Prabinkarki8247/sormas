Research profile

Public health officer at the Epidemiology and Disease Control Division, Government of Nepal, with an MPH (Distinction) from Tribhuvan University. My research focuses on the health of older adults in Nepal, including the determinants of successful aging. I have co-authored four original research articles, am a named collaborator on four Global Burden of Disease publications, and serve as a peer reviewer for seven journals. I am seeking doctoral training in epidemiology.
